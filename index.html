(03) About me

Hi, I’m Shubham.
I make brands look hungry-worthy.

I’m a graphic designer who loves the moment a product becomes a story. My work spans social media campaigns, packaging, logos and short-form motion — for snack brands, fragrance labels and jewellers who want to stand out in a crowded feed.

I blend bold typography, rich product photography and AI-assisted visuals to craft campaigns that feel premium, playful and unmistakably on-brand.
